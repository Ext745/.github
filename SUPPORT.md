# Support

These are personal projects, maintained in my own time.

- **Issues and PRs are welcome**, but responses aren't guaranteed and may take a while.
- **Check `STATUS.md` and `TESTING.md` first.** They say what works, on what hardware, and what's known to be broken.
- **No warranty.** Everything is provided as-is (see each repo's LICENSE). Hardware projects carry real risks; see `docs/hardware.md` in each repo.
- **Want a summary or explanation?** Point your AI assistant at the repo. It's structured for that (`AGENTS.md`, `GOAL.md`, `STATUS.md`, `docs/`).
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
